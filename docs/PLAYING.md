# Playing Neon Pot

## Start a table

Enter your name and select the starting chips. In solo mode, the seat selector includes you: five seats means you and four AI opponents. Use Settings → Game to adjust solo AI aggression.

For multiplayer, connect every device to the same Wi-Fi network. One player hosts and the others use Find Local Tables. The host may add AI and set each bot's aggression before the first hand. Two to six total seats are supported. Once the first hand begins, seats are locked. All players start with equal chips; later hands keep the previous results. The host starts each next hand.

## Cards and betting

- Hold your private cards to peek; release to cover them again.
- Fold, check/call, raise and all-in use the action controls. Sliding to an amount does not submit a bet.
- Raise amounts mean the total for the current betting round, including chips you already put in.
- Tap the raise amount to open the precise editor. Confirm the number, then press Raise to submit.
- Hold your chip stack for about one second on your turn to select chips manually. Tap a denomination to stage one chip; tap staged chips to take one back. Cancel returns the entire staged selection. Confirming submits it to the table.
- Exchange rearranges available denominations on your turn. It does not change your balance.
- Tap public cards and chips for cosmetic interactions; those gestures do not affect the deal.

## Settings

Switch between English and Simplified Chinese, adjust sound/music/haptics, change presentation speed and frame limit, browse card collections, draw a local custom deck or import your own music. MP3, Ogg Vorbis and WAV are supported by the local library. Windows FLAC conversion additionally needs FFmpeg. Custom drawings and imported files are not sent to other players.

The Hands guide explains fixed poker rules; it does not evaluate your current hand. There are no action timers, live odds or real-money features.

## Connection and device notes

A lost participant connection pauses the table. There is no reconnect or host migration; create a new room to continue. Keep the host active and use Wi-Fi that permits device-to-device traffic. The game uses UDP 27847 for discovery and UDP 27846 for table traffic.

Desktop mouse input acts as a single finger. Phone haptics, screen cutouts and fold/unfold behaviour depend on the device. The community package is not a claim that every device has been tested.
